Build habits that stick, with a tracker that is fast, private and fun.

DailyDots is a minimalist habit tracker and streak counter. Add a habit, tick it off, and watch your streak grow. Every check-in earns XP, so you level up from Seedling to Legend as your routine gets stronger.

WHY PEOPLE LOVE IT
- Home-screen widget: tick off today's habits without opening the app
- Streaks and milestones: 3-day, 7-day and 30-day badges keep you going
- XP and levels: small daily wins that feel rewarding
- Start in one tap: choose a starter habit and begin in seconds
- 100% offline: your data stays on your phone
- No account, no ads, no tracking
- Dark mode and Material You colors
