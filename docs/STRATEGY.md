# Practical strategy (how DailyDots can actually earn)

1. **Don't be a clone: the hook.** The store is crowded. DailyDots stands out with (a) a fast home-screen widget and (b) gamification: XP per check-in, levels (Seedling to Legend) and streak badges, in a minimal design.
2. **ASO.** Put the keywords in the title and first lines of the description (see `STORE_LISTING.md`). Lead screenshots with the widget. Ask for reviews after a 7-day streak.
3. **Retention.** Users who reach a 3 to 7 day streak are the ones who buy. The first week must be frictionless: one-tap starter habits, no sign-up, a widget that needs no app opening, and a free tier that is really usable.
4. **Expectations.** Treat v1.0 as a learning release. First goal: about 50 daily active users. Watch Day-1 / Day-7 retention and paywall views vs purchases in Play Console, then improve one thing at a time. Real profit only comes with consistent installs.

## Ideas for next versions (ordered by likely impact on retention)
- Daily reminder notification
- Streak freeze (Pro) so one missed day doesn't kill a long streak
- More badges and level titles
- Widget themes (Pro)
- Export / backup
